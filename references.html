Research notes

Sources used in this project.

The following sources provide information about the geography, geology, water systems, ecosystems, and protected areas of Chilean Patagonia. References are listed alphabetically by organization or author.

  1. Corporación Nacional Forestal. (n.d.). Parque Nacional Torres del Paine. https://www.conaf.cl/parque_nacionales/parque-nacional-torres-del-paine/
  2. Dirección Meteorológica de Chile. (n.d.). Servicios climáticos: Ficha de estación Torres del Paine. https://climatologia.meteochile.gob.cl/application/informacion/fichaDeEstacion/510012
  3. Fundación Glaciares Chilenos. (n.d.). Where can we find glaciers in Chile? https://www.glaciareschilenos.org/en/where-can-we-find-glaciers-in-chile/
  4. Hayes, G. P., Smoczyk, G. M., Benz, H. M., Furlong, K. P., & Villaseñor, A. (2015). Seismicity of the Earth 1900–2013: Seismotectonics of South America (Nazca Plate Region) (U.S. Geological Survey Open-File Report 2015–1031–E). https://doi.org/10.3133/ofr20151031E
  5. NASA. (n.d.). Earthquake in Chile. NASA Earth Observatory. https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earthquake-in-chile-19354/
  6. Servicio Nacional de Geología y Minería. (n.d.). SERNAGEOMIN. https://www.sernageomin.cl/
  7. U.S. Geological Survey. (n.d.). Understanding plate motions. https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/understanding.html
