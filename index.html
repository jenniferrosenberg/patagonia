01 / The place

A landscape shaped
by powerful forces.

At the southern end of South America, Chilean Patagonia is a place of dramatic contrasts: granite peaks, immense ice fields, deep fjords, turquoise lakes, forests, and open steppe. These landscapes are not simply beautiful. They are records of processes that have shaped our planet over millions of years.

This project follows five Earth Science themes to understand how Patagonia was formed, how it continues to change, and why its natural systems matter to people and wildlife.
