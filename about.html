A personal connection

A piece of home, seen through science.

Chile is my home country, and Patagonia is one of the places that makes me feel proud of its natural beauty. I chose the region around Torres del Paine because it brings together so many features I want to understand better: mountains, glaciers, lakes, rivers, forests, and wide open landscapes.

Through this project, I want to look beyond the beautiful views and learn about the natural processes that created them. Studying Earth Science helps me connect what I see in nature with the forces that are always working beneath and around us.

A fascinating fact

Torres del Paine National Park includes granite towers and mountain forms shaped by ancient glaciers. CONAF describes a landscape of valleys, glaciers, moraines, lakes, rivers, and waterfalls—all in one protected area.

Source: CONAF, Parque Nacional Torres del Paine.

Creation, faith, and gratitude

Alma 30:44 teaches that all things show there is a God. When I think about the mountains, the stars, the water, and the life found in Patagonia, I feel that the Earth is a beautiful testimony of His creations. Learning how these natural systems work does not take away their beauty for me. It helps me appreciate them more. I hope this project reminds me to be grateful, to care for the Earth, and to help my children see the wonder in the world around us.

“All things denote there is a God.” — Alma 30:44
